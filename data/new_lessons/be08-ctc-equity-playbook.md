## 1. Anatomy of a ₹50 LPA Indian Tech Offer

HR recruiters often headline a huge CTC figure (e.g. *"Congratulations, we are offering you ₹50 Lakhs CTC!"*). You must immediately break down this number into its 4 constituent buckets:

| Component | Example Breakdown | Real Cash in Bank (Year 1) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Base Salary** | ₹30,00,000 | ₹24,00,000 (after 30% tax + PF) | Monthly guaranteed pay. **Your next hike & bonus are calculated ONLY on this.** |
| **Annual Target Bonus** | ₹4,50,000 (15%) | ₹3,15,000 (after tax) | Discretionary, based on company & individual rating. |
| **Joining Bonus (Year 1)** | ₹5,00,000 | ₹3,50,000 (after tax) | One-time cash payment, often with 1-year clawback clause. |
| **Stock / RSUs (Year 1 portion)** | ₹10,50,000 (\$50k total / 4 yrs) | ₹7,35,000 (liquid shares) | Google/Meta: 25% each year. Amazon: 5% yr 1, 15% yr 2, 40% yr 3, 40% yr 4. |
| **Retirals (PF + Gratuity)** | ~₹1,50,000 | ₹0 (locked till retirement) | Deducted from CTC headline. |
| **Total Year 1 Headline** | **₹51,50,000** | **~₹38,00,000 real cash/stock** | |

## 2. RSUs (Public Stock) vs ESOPs (Startups)

- **RSUs (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Uber):** Publicly traded stock. As soon as a tranche vests, it is liquid cash you can sell on Nasdaq or NYSE.
- **ESOPs (Pre-IPO Startups / Swiggy / Zepto / Razorpay):** Options to buy shares at a strike price. 
  - ⚠️ **The Double Tax Trap:** You pay income tax (Perquisite Tax) on the difference between Fair Market Value (FMV) and Strike Price *upon exercise*, even if the company has not had an IPO and the shares cannot be sold!

## 3. The 3 Word-For-Word Negotiation Scripts

### Scenario 1: Recruiter asks for current salary on the first phone screen
> *"I'm under a non-disclosure agreement regarding my current compensation details. More importantly, I'm evaluating opportunities based on the scope of the Senior Engineering role at your company and market data for that level. Based on my research for L5 roles here, I am targeting a base of ₹35L–₹40L with equity. If we find a strong mutual fit in the tech rounds, I'm confident we can agree on a competitive package."*

### Scenario 2: You have an offer and want to counter
> *"Thank you so much for the offer. I am genuinely excited about the team's roadmap. While reviewing the package, I noticed the base salary is slightly below what I anticipated for an L5 scope, especially given my strong feedback in the system design round. I am also currently in final onsite rounds with another Tier-1 company where the base is anchored around ₹36L. If you can bring the base to ₹36L with a ₹6L joining bonus, I would be thrilled to sign immediately and withdraw from other loops."*
